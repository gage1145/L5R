## Summary
This ink black kanji represents oneness with all. 

## Mechanics
- When this tattoo is activated, you gain awareness of all life around you. You know the location and rough details of all living things within a radius of your School Rank x 10', even if you cannot see them. 
- You know only rough details, such as "two warriors" or "a pair of rabbits," not specific identities. You can recognize individuals well-known to you, however.
