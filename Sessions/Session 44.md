## Overview

### Meeting with [[Kitsune Hirobuna]]
Many Seppun guardsmen come out carrying the old woman. Her eyes are strikingly green which is unusual. Gaoxing offers her the gift of a poem. It's really fucking good; she liked it!

"I would be lying if I said this was an accident".

She knew we were here, and she hoped to speak with us. She knows about our meeting with her son's terrible friends. She offers the information that [[Kitsuki Kagemore]] is under her protection.

Hanzo suggests that the group could take care of her son's 'problem'. She told us that Kagemore is actually not in her immediate custody, but we did manage to convince her that we will do no harm to her son, [[Seppun Haruhisa]].

## Dealing with [[Otomo Gyobu Jensei]]
Haku attempted playing the mom out with her guitar, and a guy ran up and yelled at him for doing so. Turns out he's the judge, [[Otomo Gyobu Jensei]], who is responsible for this escort. He's pissed that we gave Hirobuna a gift and not him. Gaoxing quickly comes up with something on the fly - a song about imperial justice and the goodness of judges. He begrudgingly accepted.

[[Akodo Yashikage]] approached and apologized for Jensei. He tells us that Hirobuna wanted us to have some very well-made paint brushes.

### The Duel of the Lion and the Crane
Junji challenged Yashikage to a duel to first blood. He fucking won!

### Splitting the Group
Hanzo decides to trail hirobuna to [[Otosan Uchi]] while the other members decide to start traveling to Scorpion lands for the [[Bayushi Festival]]. Hanzo thinks that Hirobuna might try to make contact with [[Seppun Haruhisa]]. Gaoxing gives him a letter to deliver to the Tortoise.
