### Hanzo receives a letter
Hanzo

I humbly pray that the ice which thickens on the streams has not caused you hardship in your travels.  Have you managed to keep warm in your time on the road?  You shall have to tell me how the weather has treated you when next we meet.

Whilst the cold certainly makes life uncomfortable (especially the further north one travels) I cannot deny that I appreciate the respite it gives me from some of my least favourite things.  All things have a place in this world, but there is something which irks me especially about epiphytes, and it seems that flowers of this variety have become more tenacious of late; their beauty seems only to exist because of the strength of their host – even if their blooms come in bright colours.  I once saw an epiphyte which blossomed in the most brilliant shades of pink, yet its petals only distracted from the flower it grew upon, whose petals of white and gold struggled under the tax its passenger enacted on it.  As the season aged, I heard more samurai speak of the epiphyte, and fewer of the host flower, until it seemed only I remembered which blossom held the original, true beauty.  I spoke only of this to my nearest servants, so as not to embarrass those whose tastes I found so boorish, and was tempted occasionally to trim the flower myself.  Yet after weeks of this frustration, one day I passed by the spot where the flowers had grown together, and saw only the original; there was no sign of the other.  It seems the kami shared my tastes.

On to more pressing matters, I would like to invite you to join me at [[Sanroku Tenshu]].  I have been told of your heroism in defending the Badger’s honour, and greatly wish to hear of your experiences.  From what I have heard, and of our brief meeting, I believe you may be able to help me resolve a small trade issue.  It is of no great importance, if other matters are pressing, but I would consider it a great personal favour, and an opportunity to learn more from your wisdom.

I have included travel papers to [[Kyuden Tonbo]] and sent word to the Tonbo that you and your fellows are arriving as my guests and should be provided for, as a kindness to their neighbour.  [[Tonbo Baiko]] is an excellent host and I am certain you shall find your time there pleasant.  You shall unfortunately, since you travel in mixed company, have to wait for a representative of the Kitsuki to arrive to vet your party.  I do not know who is likely to represent their interests, but I am certain that if you tell the absolute truth you will encounter no issue.

I look forwards to our conversations, and shall share a tea with you when you arrive that I have had imported from Faroshima.  I have not yet sampled it, but I am told that it was obtained at great personal cost by a valiant Yoritomo bushi and that I am the first lord in the mainland outside of Otosan Uchi to have purchased it.  Depending on the length of your stay, perhaps we can share it with my beloved friend, [[Mirumoto Masamune]]-donno-sama, who I understand means to pay me a visit.

Respectfully,

[[Mirumoto Naomori]]

18th year of the 36th Hantei, 10th month, 25th day
