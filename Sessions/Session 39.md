## Overview
### Hanzo Rescues [[Eichi Jin]]
While the others are getting information on the local ronin, Hanzo goes back to the village to rescue Jin and/or assassinate [[Kitsuki Kagemore]]. Kagemore wasn't there, but he did manage to free Jin.

On the way back, Jin requests that we go back to get his armor, and Hanzo says that once we meet back up with his companions.

Jin suggests there may be ~40 ronin in the local camp. During the day, there are 4 on watch and 2 patrols of 4. Night,  3 on watch, 3 patrols of 3.

Gaoxing convinces [[Togashi Morai]] to join us in taking care of the bandits.

### Infiltrating the Bandit Camp
We manage to get in and hide pretty well. Hanzo hears that they are talking about Jin. They are convinced he may be a ghost.

We used Gaoxing as bait along with the big barrells of high proof sake that Jin had stashed.

We quickly dispatched 5 of them. After that, we engaged in battle with the rest of them.

We won the battle lol

### Talking with [[Kitsuki Kentaro 2]]
After a few days, Kentaro finds us in the village, and says we are charged with espionage, subterfuge, etc.

We get the sense that he doesn't really believe any of  these charges, but that he was sent to serve the warrant by [[Kitsuki Kagemore]]. Gaoxing argues that the source of the warrant may be faulty. We could prove that there is a plot to smuggle foreign guns using Empire crates labelled as spears.

We manage to convince him to let us go without charging us, and we must go back to deliver the message about rice imports to [[Mirumoto Naomori]].

As we finish going through the rubble, we find shipping instructions from a guy named [[Ooka]] in [[Otosan Uchi]].
