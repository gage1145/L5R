## Overview

### Deciding where to go
We can either go find [[Koganshi Onotatsu]] and have him rejoin our group or continue to [[Otosan Uchi]]. [[Heichi Jin]] decided he will travel with us to Otosan Uchi.

### Traveling through Lion Lands
We arrive in [[Rugashi Toshi]], a bustling city! It is a major trade hub for the Dragon and Lion clans. We found lodging, and we overheard some samurai. They are [[Rokugo Family]] samurai - members of the family that Onotatsu allegedly murdered. The woman, [[Rokugo Raiko]], introduces herself as Haku approaches her. Haku brings up the fact that we met Onotatsu's sister.

As we continue talking, she kind of picks up on the fact that we know Onotatsu thanks to Junji being a bad liar. She lays out a paper showing a warrant for Onotatsu, signed by [[Rokugo Onomoro]].

Hanzo ordered a round of shoju, and made a toast **"to duty!"**

Junji challenges [[Rokugo Tarahaki]] to a duel. It turns into a drinking contest, and Junji is almost certainly guaranteed to blow chunks.

### The Duel
Junji and Tarahaki go out into the street to duel. This, after he challenged Hanzo, but Hanzo was forced to offer Junji as his champion since he doesn't use swords.

Junji quickly slices a giant gash in his arm, but the Scorpion fucker didn't even draw. Junji forfeited because he was so weirded out. Hanzo lost some glory for that one lol.

Junji drank with Jin and Tarahaki until the sun came up.

### Otosan Uchi
This place is fucking enormous! Yoshi gets to flex his fame! Took us 2 weeks to reach.

Yakisoba asked around and found out that a merchant named [[Ooka]] was murdered 2 weeks ago. Hanzo tries to ask around about Ooka's employer, but doesn't get anywhere. Yoshi finds out that, at the very least, Ooka had a rival named [[Toyaharu]]. 

We look for lodging near the dock and Yoshi pays a koku for it. 

At some point a man comes in. The inn-keep points him in our direction. He's completely bald, got a gun! This guy is a member of the [[Tortoise Clan]]. He introduces himself as [[Kasuga Kenjaro]]. This guy is part of basically a criminal organization that is ordained by the emperor.
