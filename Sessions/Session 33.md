## Overview
We start after killing the whole village. I missed a bunch of zombies, and Hanzo lost an eye. [[Akoma Takashi]] suggested taking the cursed sword wielded by a zombie, burying it in an undisclosed location, and killing himself. We figured that was a bit much, so Hanzo took the sword along with some jade for protection and vowed to return it to Phoenix lands to be destroyed.

### Hunting peasants
Yoshi and Yaki go peasant hunting just in case there are stragglers from the tainted village. Yaki kills 30 peasants.

### Traveling to Phoenix Lands
Along the road, we come across some Lion samurai. [[Akodo Yobu Munatare]] introduces himself. He tall af, 8ft. He asks for our papers. This guy questioned us for fucking ever, and eventually we moved on.

Eventually we cross into Phoenix lands. We pass by the burial site of [[Emperor Hantei XVII]]. Within the castle [[Aojiro Oku Shiro]], we met [[Isawa Masahira]]. He welcomed us in and introduced us to his wife [[Sukimi]]. Gaoxing immediately asks to speak to a high-ranking shugenja. Sukimi, being that shugenja, offers to meet Gaoxing and Hanzo in private. We show her the cursed sword that Hanzo had been carrying. She assures us there is a solution, but we should probably meet with [[Isawa Kyoto]] when she arrives from her trip.

We meet a fuck ton of people. Too many to list here.

We met with Isawa Kyoto to show her the sword. Hanzo does a cool kata with it, and she seems unimpressed. We find out she is definitely part of the [[Kyōtsū no michi]].
