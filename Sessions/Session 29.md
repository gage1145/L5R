## Overview

The group reconvenes after getting the letter from [[Akoma Takashi]]. 

### Sneaking into the forest
Yoshiyuki, Yakisoba, and Hanzo attempt to sneak into the forest to investigate the bandit reports. Unfortunately, the two former were caught and had to pretend to be drunk to get out of being found out.

### The Hunter's Hut
Gaoxing and Haku approached the hunter's hut. It is covered in animal skins, and the two samurai found it very off-putting. Gaoxing walks in and tries to wake up the hunter. He asks, "Why are you here, samurai?" Gaoxing asks him about the woods and to take him to see where the bandits are camped.

### The Scream
While the others are in the forest, Junji stayed behind to watch [[Shizu]]'s house. After an hour, he hears a loud scream, so he burst in and found Shizu with a knife in his chest. Shizu warned us that they were going to kill him...Another bushi came in to investigate, and dismissed Junji. He seems to think the peasant might have killed himself. Junji thinks it would be weird if someone screamed before killing themselves.

Junji waits for them to leave, then sneaks back into the house. From what he can tell, there was no struggle.

After a while, a messenger approaches Junji and says that Takashi wants to speak with him. As he's walking out the house, he's ambushed. When one reaches out and touches him, it feels like his veins are turning to stone.

### Traveling to the Ruins
While traveling to the ruins, we came across a man camping. He had been cursed, and could only speak nonsense. We find out he is the real [[Akoma Takashi]] from the village. We try to find out what happened, and he went to the fire, grabbed a cold piece of ash, put it to his heart, and let go. We have no idea what that means. Eventually, Haku figures out that [[Shinroku]], was the maho-sukai that cursed the samurai. [[Togi]] is impersonating him.

After a while, a woman with a shaved head approaches and speaks some nonsense to him. This was the temple maiden. We find out that we will likely need to slaughter the village. There are also likely some villagers imprisoned at the ruins a way's away.

### Sneaking back to the village
Yoshiyuki and Hanzo returned to get jade from the smithy to prevent the taint from spreading in Junji. They killed a guard quickly and silently.
