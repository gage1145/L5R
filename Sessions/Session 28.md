## Overview
### The Happenings of [[Mushi Mura]]
Yakisoba is acting kinda weird. Probably something to do with that note he received. The trek is quiet on the way to Mushi Mura; in fact, it's too quiet. We see basically no signs of human life on this road. No travellers, nothing. As we approached the village, we could see distant villagers in the rice fields. We don't really sense any danger, otherwise. We look for [[Akoma Takashi]] who was put in charge of the village. Soon we see a well-dressed samurai coming down from the large house at the top of the hill.

 He (Takashi) says that there is no problem here, and we should turn back. We tell him that we'll let [[Akodo Hoshimitsu]] that he turned us away. He changes his tune and agrees to give us a tour of the town. Hanzo and Yakisoba sneak off independently without Takashi noticing.

One thing we notice about this village is that there are no children or elderly here. Also, some of the houses seem uninhabited. It's pretty obvious the villagers are frightened. There was one girl that actually sprinted away when she saw Takashi coming. Another villager complained that someone named "Shizu" isn't pulling her weight.

Gaoxing suggests that Takashi writes a letter himself stating that all is well in this town. This man is acting very cagey, but he doesn't seem to be possessed or anything.
