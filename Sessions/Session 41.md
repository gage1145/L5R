## Overview
### Learning more about [[Ooka]]
- He sold surplus from inbound traders
- He was likely running a fence and was in direct competition with [[Toyaharu]].
- Multiple people saw a turtle say that he was going to kill him.
- He was completely blacklisted by the tortoise.
- There is a rumor that two Tortoises visited [[Seppun Sanasuke]], and they were thrown out for threatening his life.
- Sanasuke was a frequent buyer from Ooka.
- Sanasuke is the Imperial accountant for the region.
- Sanasuke earlier this year lodged a formal request to the emperor for a firearm regiment to use against a Lion lord.

After asking around a bit, a porter approaches Yoshi with a letter. It's sealed with the Tortoise clan mon. Yoshi is very bashful about what this letter was about.

### The Sake House
Hanzo asked about the owner of the house. It is some random business owner, unlikely to be involved with our investigation. From the bartender, we learn the Tortoise serve as a mild authority here, butthe [[Seppun Family]] are the true authorities.

A drunk Tortoise tells us that [[Kasuga Kenjaro]] hangs out with [[Kasuga Izo]]. We also learn that the guns that Ooka was pedaling were [[Eisan]] guns (i.e. foreign Gaijin guns). We can go find a guy named [[Kasuga Naoyuki]] and try to find this drunk fuck's name. We can find Naoyuki.

### Meeting Again with Kenjaro
Kenjaro let's us know that our drunk friend commited seppuku for carrying an illegal firearm. He told us to get the fuck out of dodge, and he knows that we were the last ones to talk to him. He offers us papers to leave to Crane lands. We instead offer him information about the Imperial gun trade.

We go with Kenjaro to meet with [[Kasuga Izo]]. Izo asks about the information we are willing to offer. We tell him about the link to [[Kitsuki Kagemore]] and his buying of firearms from Ooka and Seppun Sanasuke.

He tells us that Ooka somehow gained knowledge of this shipment (guns) heading to Sanasuke. He sold them to [[Akoma Shimoi]]. Sanasuke had requested and was denied due to a personal feud. The tortoise asked him for his side of the story but he refused and kicked them out.

### Talking with Toyaharu
We find this jabroni hocking a bunch of Gaijin bullshit. Junji brings up Ooka, and he spits at the sound of his name. We didn't really gain any other useful information.
